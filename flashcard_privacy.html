Privacy Policy for Splashcards

Last updated: 4 November, 2026

NordicTechTeam AB respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Splashcards app.

1. App Provider

Splashcards is developed and provided by:

NordicTechTeam AB
Sweden
Email: nordictechteam@gmail.com

2. Information Stored in the App

When using Splashcards, you can create decks and flashcards containing, for example, questions, answers, and images.

Decks, flashcards, and related information are stored locally on your device.

NordicTechTeam AB does not automatically have access to the contents of your decks or flashcards, and this content is not sent to NordicTechTeam AB's servers.

3. Images

Splashcards allows you to select images from your device and use them in flashcards.

The app only accesses images that you select yourself using your device's image picker.

Images added to Splashcards are stored locally together with the app's other information. They are not uploaded to NordicTechTeam AB's servers.

4. Microphone and Speech Recognition

Splashcards includes speech recognition features. If you choose to use these features, the app may need access to your device's microphone and speech recognition capabilities.

The microphone is used only when you activate a feature that requires speech recognition and after you have granted the necessary permissions.

Splashcards uses speech recognition services provided by your device or operating system. Depending on your device, operating system, and settings, audio or speech may therefore be processed by the provider of that service, such as Apple or Google.

Such processing is governed by the relevant service provider's terms and privacy policy.

NordicTechTeam AB does not receive or store recordings of your speech on its own servers.

5. Text-to-Speech

Splashcards may use your device's text-to-speech functionality to read aloud text from flashcards.

This functionality is provided by the device's or operating system's speech service. Depending on the device and its settings, processing may be subject to the service provider's own terms and privacy policy.

6. Study Statistics

Splashcards may store information about how you use your decks in order to display study statistics, such as results from completed study sessions.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to NordicTechTeam AB.

7. Import and Export

Splashcards includes features for importing and exporting information, such as decks.

Splashcards allows you to create and restore backups. You choose where to save a backup. Backups are not automatically sent to NordicTechTeam AB.

When you choose to export information, you decide where the exported file is saved or shared.

After export, the service or storage location you choose is responsible for any further processing of the information.

8. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Splashcards does not use advertisements to fund the app.

Splashcards does not use information from the app for targeted advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

NordicTechTeam AB does not currently use third-party analytics services to collect information about how you use Splashcards.

9. Sharing and Selling Information

NordicTechTeam AB does not sell information about Splashcards users.

Information may be processed by services provided by your device's operating system when you use features such as speech recognition, text-to-speech, or file management.

10. Storage and Deletion

Information saved by Splashcards is stored locally on your device.

You can delete decks and flashcards using the app's features.

Information may also be removed by deleting the app's data or uninstalling the app, depending on how your device's operating system handles local storage.

11. Children

Splashcards is a general study and learning app and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Because users decide what content to enter into their flashcards, we recommend that sensitive or unnecessary personal information not be stored in the app.

12. Security

Splashcards is designed primarily to store user information locally on the user's own device and to limit access to device features to what is necessary for the app's functionality.

The security of information stored on the device also depends on the device's operating system and the user's own security settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Splashcards' features or the way information is handled change.

The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

14. Contact

If you have questions about Splashcards or this Privacy Policy, you can contact:

NordicTechTeam AB
Sweden
